# Preparation and future checkpoint lifecycle

Current authorization is a finite preparation/qualification stage. No new GPU run, installation, waiting launcher or Judge consumer is created in this stage. The original146 active owner101/step1 state stays retained and paused; C/O/L retains its original completion barrier. Their clocks and budgets are unchanged.

The preparation audit reads historical source annotations, the 61 frozen BASIS rows, actual old Base response prefixes, original146 evaluation input identities (no evaluation answers), and the first100 continuous update events. It writes only a new private preparation record and anonymous qualification evidence. There is one collision check; a failed full buffer is BLOCKED, never silently repaired or reduced. Source questions, answers, images, token prefixes, identity maps and per-step schedules remain private.

Any later executable must use a separate run and its own activation clock. Preserve FP32 master, native FP16 W, Python/NumPy/Torch/CUDA RNG, source/protection clock, counters and events. Checkpoint20 saving and cooperative stop may be implemented only after equivalent recovery checks; all actual replay cost is counted. A failed generation/Judge attempt is never implicitly repeated.

Only physical GPU5 on pro5000 is allowed. The final100 weight must stay until a new process verifies registered exact native reload outputs and closes its GPU lease. Only explicitly owned obsolete matrices may be retired with receipts after their consumers finish; original146, C/O/L and historical assets remain read-only. No inference parameters or branches may be added.
